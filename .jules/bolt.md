## 2024-06-25 - Integer Root Optimization and Hex Filter Modulo
**Learning:** In C#, replacing integer binary search implementations for square roots and cube roots with hardware-accelerated floating-point operations (`Math.Sqrt` and `Math.Cbrt`) results in >30x and >4x speedups respectively. Because `double` precision can lose exactness for large 64-bit integers, an exact correction step is necessary to guarantee correct bounds at `long.MaxValue`. Additionally, a "hex filter" modulo 64 using bitwise masking (`x & 63` and `0x0202021202030213L`) allows instantly eliminating ~81.25% of invalid candidates for perfect squares, speeding up `IsPerfectSquare` significantly (17x for non-perfect squares).
**Action:** When asked to optimize integer arithmetic routines or checks, always verify if hardware-accelerated `Math` floating-point approximations combined with a fast exactness check/correction are faster than pure integer binary searches. Use modulo bit masks to quickly filter out impossible candidates.
